Kūhaku

Make this computer Kūhaku.

Kūhaku will replace everything on this computer's drive with a calm, empty screen and one line.

It takes about ten minutes.

You

Your name and one password.

The password opens the computer when it starts. You will not be asked again after that.

You can change this later in Settings.

Programs

What would you like?

Choose what appears on this computer. Programs by others are fetched from their makers once you are online. Claude can add or remove any of them later.
